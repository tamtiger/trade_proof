# Fixtures

This directory separates real anonymized samples from synthetic fixtures.

## Real Binance CSV Samples

Current status: `0/5` real samples collected.

Real samples are not committed until consent and anonymization records exist. See [Fixture Intake](../docs/operations/fixture-intake.md).

## Synthetic Fixtures

Synthetic fixtures may be added under `fixtures/synthetic/` for deterministic parser and boundary checks. They must not be described as real Binance exports and do not satisfy the real-sample gate.
